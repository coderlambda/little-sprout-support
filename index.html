Grow a little farm.
Learn a little every day.

Little Sprout Farm combines a relaxed 3D farm with optional math practice. Plant crops, feed animals, make farm products, fill orders, and care for roaming pets.

A farm that grows with you

Upgrade your farmhouse, open new workshops, and manage production queues. Spend Spirit Coins earned through play and practice to help your farm.

Practice chosen by parents

Parents can choose math topics and daily question counts, then review answers, scores and progress on the device. The game supports English and Simplified Chinese.

Common questions

Does it need an account or internet?
No account is needed. Farming and math practice work offline.

Where is my progress saved?
On your device. There is no in-game cloud account or cross-device sync. Deleting the app and its data removes local progress; offloading keeps it.

How do I choose practice topics?
Open Settings, unlock the parent controls, and choose Practice Setup or Daily Setup. The local parent password is created on first use.

How do I earn Spirit Coins?
Use the plus button beside the Spirit Coin balance to start practice. Tasks and orders can also award coins.

Support / 支持

For help with Little Sprout Farm, open a support issue. Describe the screen, the steps that led to the problem, and your app version and device model. Support issues are public: please do not include passwords, contact details, or children’s personal information. An adult should send support requests.
