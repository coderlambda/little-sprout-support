Privacy Policy
隐私政策

Updated October 24, 2026 · 更新日期：2026 年 10 月 24 日

Little Sprout Farm works offline. No account is required. The game does not send your farm or learning records to the developer.

小芽农场可离线使用，无需注册账号。游戏不会向开发者发送农场或学习记录。

Stored on your device: farm progress, inventory, pets, preferences, practice answers and results, daily assignments, and a salted hash of the parent password. These records provide gameplay, learning progress and parental controls.

保存在设备上的信息包括：农场进度、库存、宠物、设置、练习答案与成绩、每日任务及家长密码的加盐哈希。这些信息用于游戏运行、学习统计和家长管理。

There are no ads, tracking tools, analytics SDKs, in-app purchases or in-app chat. The game does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos. Device backups and diagnostics that you choose to share with Apple follow your Apple settings.

游戏没有广告、跟踪工具、分析 SDK、内购或聊天功能，不会请求访问通讯录、定位、相机、麦克风或照片。设备备份以及您选择向 Apple 共享的诊断信息由 Apple 的相关设置管理。

Records stay on this device until you reset the relevant learning records or delete the app and its data. Offloading the app keeps its data. Device or iCloud backups may retain copies under your Apple backup settings. We do not keep a separate server copy.

记录保留在此设备上，直到您重置相关学习记录或删除应用及其数据。“卸载 App”会保留数据。设备或 iCloud 备份可能依照 Apple 备份设置保留副本。我们不另行保存服务器副本。

Parents can protect learning settings with a local password. No child name, email address or online profile is required. The game does not collect personal information from children for advertising or profiling.

家长可以使用本地密码保护学习设置。游戏无需儿童姓名、邮箱或在线档案，不会收集儿童个人信息用于广告或用户画像。

Support and the current policy: coderlambda.github.io/little-sprout-support/. If you choose to open a support issue on GitHub, its contents are public and handled under GitHub's privacy policy. Please have an adult request support and omit children's personal details.

支持与最新政策：coderlambda.github.io/little-sprout-support/。如您选择在 GitHub 提交支持问题，其内容会公开并适用 GitHub 隐私政策。请由成年人申请支持，不要填写儿童个人信息。

This support website / 支持网站

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not use advertising or analytics scripts. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to operate its hosting service. If you create a support issue, GitHub stores your account information and the public content you submit under its privacy statement. You can edit or delete your issue content using GitHub and request help through the support page. Do not submit sensitive information.

本静态网站托管于 GitHub Pages，不使用广告或分析脚本。GitHub 可能为提供托管服务处理 IP 地址等技术请求信息。提交支持问题时，GitHub 按其隐私声明保存账号信息及公开内容。您可以通过 GitHub 编辑或删除问题内容，并通过支持页面寻求帮助。请勿提交敏感信息。

Support / 支持

For help with Little Sprout Farm, open a support issue. Describe the screen, the steps that led to the problem, and your app version and device model. Support issues are public: please do not include passwords, contact details, or children’s personal information. An adult should send support requests.

需要帮助时，请提交支持问题，说明遇到问题的界面、操作步骤、应用版本和设备型号。支持问题会公开，请由成年人提交，不要填写密码、联系方式或儿童个人信息。
